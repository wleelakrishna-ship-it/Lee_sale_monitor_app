# Privacy Policy — LEE Sale Monitor

**Last updated:** 25 November 2026

LEE Sale Monitor ("the app", "we", "us") is a daily sales tracking tool for fish
sellers and small businesses. This privacy policy explains what information the
app handles and how it is used.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** When you sign in, we use your email address and a
  password to authenticate you through Firebase Authentication (Google). We do not
  receive or store your password in readable form.
- **Business data you enter:** fish categories and rates, sales records, buyer
  names, payment amounts and balances, expenses, investor and profit‑share data,
  and similar records you create in the app.
- **Device and technical data:** limited technical information needed to operate
  cloud sync and backups (such as an app/installation identifier). We do not use
  advertising identifiers.

## How we use information

- To provide the app's features: recording sales, calculating totals and balances,
  generating reports and managing investors/expenses.
- To authenticate you and keep your data synced and backed up across your devices.
- To restore your data when you change phones or reinstall the app.

We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** use it for advertising.

## Where data is stored

Business data is stored locally on your device and, when you are signed in, backed
up to a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database (Google Cloud) under your own account.
Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to cloud data is
restricted to authenticated users of your account via row-level security.

## Data sharing

We do not share your personal or business data with third parties, except:
- **Service providers:** Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) processes
  data on our behalf to run the app.
- **Legal requirements:** if required by applicable law.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains available while your account is active. You can:
- **Export a backup** at any time from the app.
- **Request deletion** of your account and associated cloud data by contacting us
  at the email below. We will delete the data within a reasonable period, except
  where retention is required by law.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Security

We use industry‑standard measures, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and
authenticated access, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage
is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will
reflect changes. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the
updated policy.
