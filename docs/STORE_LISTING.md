## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

```
LEE Sale Monitor is a simple, fast daily sales tracker built for fish sellers and
small wholesale businesses. Record what you sell each day, track what each buyer
still owes, and see your totals at a glance — no paperwork, no spreadsheets.

KEY FEATURES

• Today's Overview — see total kilograms sold, total sales amount, commission and
  net amount for the day, plus a per-buyer breakdown with outstanding balances.

• New Sale — pick a fish category and rate, enter the kilograms, and the amount is
  calculated instantly. Add multiple items to a cart, record the amount the buyer
  paid, and save the whole sale in one tap.

• Rates Setup — set your daily buying/selling rate and commission per kilogram for
  each fish category. Rates are reused automatically when you enter a new sale.

• Buyers & Balances — keep a running balance for every buyer. Record part-payments
  and instantly see who still owes money and how much.

• Reports — view sales history and totals over any date range to understand your
  business performance.

• Investors & Profit Share — track investor contributions, expenses and profit
  share so everyone's numbers stay clear.

• Backup & Restore — export a full backup of your data and restore it on a new
  phone, so your records are never locked to one device.

• Cloud Sync — your data is backed up to the cloud and synced across devices when
  you sign in.

• English & Tamil — switch the app language at any time.

• Works offline — keep recording sales without a connection; data syncs when you're
  back online.

WHO IT'S FOR
Fish sellers, market vendors and small wholesale traders who want a clear daily
record of sales, payments and outstanding balances.

PRIVACY
Your data is used only to provide the app's features. See the privacy policy for
details.
```
